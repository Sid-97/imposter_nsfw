3–10 players · One phone · Trust no one

The rules of deception

How to play

1

Get your role

Pass the phone around. Everyone sees the same secret word—except one sneaky imposter.

2

Give a clue

Take turns saying just one word that describes the secret. Be helpful, but don’t give it away!

3

Find the imposter

Keep doing clue rounds or call a vote. Catch the imposter before they blend in.

★ Crewmates win by voting out the imposter. The imposter wins by fooling everyone.
